## Gaps and duplicates

- **Essence (132-140) has no sheet** in the 43-sheet plan. The cards are drawn as vector designs by the card renderer. If painted Essence art is wanted, add a sheet (9 cells).
- **Five scene sheets already exist outside the numbering** (`art-source/scenes/`: Astral, Stone, Tide, Verdant, Wonder; 45 backgrounds). They are registered as the background library for creature cards of those affinities. The bible flags Dusk and Iron backgrounds as missing; Ember and Storm are missing too, so Biomes B and C are assigned to those four.
- **Biome cards 111-119** need Biomes A. Until it is approved, the five biomes that have a background library show one of those scenes, labelled interim; the other four show a placeholder.
- **Already delivered creature sheets** (`art-source/creatures/`, approved earlier): Cactuft (all 9 poses, sheet 01 complete), Flapod (all 9, sheet 10 complete), Bashlet, Rolyte and Frondolin (9 poses of the Basic only; their family sheets 03, 05, 06 still need Stage 1 and Stage 2). Those sheets are queued in full so the stages match; approved cells are never overwritten (`tools/wildkin_art.py`).
- **The koi sheet** (`art-source/creatures/koi-poses-green.png`) shows one koi-dragon form of the Koilet -> Runakoi -> Koirune line. Which stage it is was never recorded, so it is **not** mapped to a card. Once the owner says which stage, approve it with `python3 tools/wildkin_art.py legacy --koi <stage card number>`.
- **Cactuft chase examples** (`art-source/chase-examples/cactuft-fullart-examples.png`, three paintings) are examples, not assigned to 141, 151 or 173. They can be submitted through `incoming/` if the owner wants them used.
- **Design explorations** (dolphin-cloud, pastel axolotl, fluffy llama) are not assigned to any card (bible section 3).
- **No 3-stage family sheet for the Mistip line is delivered**; Nimbusail needs it before its IR (161) and SIR (174).
- **Art direction belongs to SOL.** Only 5 creatures (Cactuft, Bashlet, Rolyte, Frondolin, Flapod) are drawn in this repository. SOL designs every other creature, character, object and the Clasp from its earlier Wildkin art. No concept approval step: the first approved sheet of a subject becomes its reference, and later pictures (other poses, premium art) must match it.
- **Premium resolution.** A 1254 px 3x3 sheet gives 418 px cells, too small for full-art cards. Premium sheets must be at least 2100 x 2940 px (700 x 980 per cell).
