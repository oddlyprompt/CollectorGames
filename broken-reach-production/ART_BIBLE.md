# The Broken Reach: art bible (proposal, 2026-10-30)

Source: the owner's handoff (`source/`), v0.1. Everything here is **proposed** until the owner approves it (gate G4 for lore, G5 for master designs). Nothing in it is canon.

## Tone and look
Mature, lived-in space opera: cinematic realism with painterly character. Palette: gunmetal, oxblood and amber with cool-cyan accents. Functional, worn technology; varied environments; culturally diverse alien physiques. A fractured political order after the Sundering (the Lattice gate network partly collapsed 43 years ago).
Avoid: repetitive front-facing weapon poses, identical camera compositions, generic humans with recoloured skin, generic sleek spaceship silhouettes, anything that reads as a stock render.

## No text on the picture
No text, borders, rarity icons or directional arrows in any picture. The game draws the frame, the arrows, the stats and the ability.

## Format (owner's direction, 2026-10-31)
**Base cards: square (1:1). Premium arts: portrait (about 3:4). Master design sheets: landscape.** PNG preferred; the shortest side at least 400 px (the owner waived the earlier 1600 px minimum; larger is better). The base card shows the square picture in a square window, a premium card shows its portrait picture nearly full-card, so **keep the main action inside the central 70%** and do not put essential detail near an edge. A ship needs a unique, identifiable silhouette that reads at thumbnail size.

## Species (canon anatomy from the handoff)
- **Orren**: small, agile, tawny fur, expressive ears.
- **Dravak**: heavy, plated, four-eyed, built for high gravity.
- **Vaelari**: elegant cranial structures, bioluminescent markings.
- **Selyth**: amphibious, blue-green, wear external suits.
- **Khepri**: six-limbed, chitin.
- **Thessari**: crystalline beings in mobility frames.
Affiliation is not species: any species can belong to any faction.

## The cast (face consistency matters most here; master design sheets come first)
- **Kaia Venn**: 23-year-old human ace pilot, messy dark auburn hair, freckles, brazen grin, worn burgundy flight jacket
- **Tavi**: small gentle tawny-furred Orren ship enthusiast, long expressive ears, amber eyes, utility overalls, carefully handling complex ship machinery
- **Dorrak**: young broad Dravak recruit with layered stony dermal plates and four eyes, modest battered guard armor, sincere imposing stance
- **Elias Voss**: 27-year-old human male systems engineer, short dark hair, reserved gaze, austere modular technical jacket, active diagnostic projection
- **Lyssara Vale**: striking confident adult Vaelari woman, charcoal-opalescent skin, elegant cranial ridges, discreet bioluminescent markings, bold expensive tailored attire
- **Neru Sael**: aloof blue-green amphibious Selyth inventor in ingenious articulated transparent circulation exosuit, visible fluid valves and adaptive limbs
- **Silas Veyne**: older poised human crime lord, silver hair, tailored oxblood suit, gold cybernetic details and an unreadable expression
- **Rix Vanno**: vain violet-toned Vaelari syndicate upstart, extravagant crimson tailoring and gold jewelry, smug look, visibly overdecorated tech

## Faction look (proposed)
- **Free Captains**: patched freighters, dock gear, amber worklights, improvised repairs.
- **Veyne Exchange**: oxblood and gold, contraband signage, smuggling stations, tailored crime-lord elegance.
- **Concordance Remnant**: faded steel-blue uniforms and ships of a collapsed order, regulation but neglected.
- **Veyr Consortium**: cold violet-grey corporate glass and audit machinery.
- **Quiet Assembly**: dim cyan, ritual and listening, gate chapels.
- **Independent**: no house style; let the subject decide.

## Planets, ships, premium arts
- **Planets** (8): spectacular lived-in establishing illustrations, no characters in focus.
- **Ships**: each its own silhouette, engines and plating visible; vary the camera (see the shot in each queue item).
- **Premium arts** (27: 16 Illustration, 8 Signature, 3 Eclipse): a different moment, composition and setting from the base picture. Eclipse is the most dramatic painting of the set.

## Production order
Master design sheets first (the eight characters and six species, batch M00): the owner approves them before any card art. Then batches of at most 12 (main cast, Legendary, Super Rare, planets, Rare, Uncommon, Common), then the premium arts in batches of nine. See `ART_QUEUE.md` and `art_queue.json`.
