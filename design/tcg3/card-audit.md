# Rift Parade Set 01: card audit

Generated by `node tools/rift-parade/audit.js --write --sim <results.json>`. **130 cards audited; 130 pass every check; 0 fail.** The same checks run inside `node tools/rift-parade/check.js`.

Every card is checked for: valid data; canonical identity fields present (the exact text of names, rarities, types, episodes and art directions is verified against the production CSV in `check.js`); deck-building rules (3 copies, 1 for Secret Rares); implemented keywords; implemented effects; generated rules text; legal targets in a built position; resolution under the engine; an observable effect when played; and, for **every ability**, that it can be triggered (triggers fire in their own scenario, activated abilities are offered and resolve, countdowns count down and resolve, static effects change stats, keywords or player rules on a built board).

Positions are built by `audit.js` (`rich()`): both players have boards, Relics, hands, discard piles, a Zone and a Rift of 4, so conditional abilities can fire. Complex abilities also have hand-written interaction tests in `check.js`.

## Cards that are intentionally situational

These cards need a particular board and are not meant to be played in every game: Reaction Techniques (Momentum Skip, Hard Stop, Redirected Blow, Last-Second Detour, Prime-Time Switch, Resonance Shelter, Kinetic Reversal, Resonance Overflow) need an attack; Clean Sever and Bondbreaker Stroke need an opposing Condition, Relic or Zone; Weak-Point-style kill effects (Ghostline Guillotine, Toma, One Perfect Answer) need a Marked or flawed target; House Advantage and Loaded Coin only help Wager decks; Live on Air and Mistaken Accomplice need an opposing Character; Equivalent Exchange and Dmg-by-Relic effects need a spare Relic.
