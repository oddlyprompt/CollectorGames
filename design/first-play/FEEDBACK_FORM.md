# Trading Card Collector: first-play feedback

Fill this in straight after playing, before talking it over. Short answers are fine. "I don't know" is a useful answer.
Where there is a scale, 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very much".

## 1. First impressions
1. In one sentence, what is this game about?
2. What did you do in your first five minutes?
3. Was there a moment early on when you did not know what to do? What were you looking at?
4. How did the game look and feel on your device? Anything hard to read or hard to tap?

## 2. The core loop
5. Describe a typical day in the game in your own words.
6. How clear was it what you could do each day? 1 2 3 4 5
7. Did moving time forward yourself (Next day, Skip) feel right, too slow, or too fast? Why?
8. Did you always understand why your money went up or down? If not, when not?

## 3. Opening packs
9. How much did you enjoy opening packs? 1 2 3 4 5
10. Describe the best pack or card you opened. Why that one?
11. After opening, did you know which of your new cards mattered to you? How?
12. Anything about opening that felt slow, confusing or flat?

## 4. Shops
13. Which shops did you visit? Name the ones you remember.
14. Did they feel like different places? 1 2 3 4 5. What made them different, if anything?
15. Did you have a favourite, or one you avoided? Why?
16. Did you notice anything about how a shop prices its cards?
17. Did you notice your standing with a shop? Did it matter to you?
18. Was there something you wanted to buy or sell and could not? What stopped you?

## 5. Out & about: yard sales, collections, the card show
19. Did you go to a yard sale, look at a collection or go to a card show? Which?
20. How much did you enjoy it? 1 2 3 4 5 (leave blank if you did not go)
21. How did you decide whether to buy? What did you wish you knew?
22. Did making an offer feel like a real decision?
23. Did "two outings a day" ever make you choose between things? Was that good or annoying?
24. If you did not go to any: did you see them? What kept you away?

## 6. Wanting things
25. Did you end up with a goal of your own? What was it, and when did it form?
26. How much did you care about your collection by the end? 1 2 3 4 5
27. Which of the three card games drew you most? Why? Was there one you ignored?
28. Did the game help you chase what you wanted, or get in the way?

## 7. Friction
29. What did you do over and over that you would rather not have?
30. What was the most frustrating moment? What were you trying to do?
31. Were there waits that bothered you? Roughly how long, and when?
32. Any word, number or screen you did not understand?
33. Anything that looked broken?

## 8. Coming back
34. If the session had not ended, would you have kept playing? 1 2 3 4 5
35. What would you do first if you opened it again tomorrow?
36. Would you show it to a friend? What would you tell them it is?
37. What is the one thing you would change?
38. What is the one thing we must not change?

## Two questions to answer aloud
- What surprised you most?
- Is there anything you expected to be able to do that you could not?
