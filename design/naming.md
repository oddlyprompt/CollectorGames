# Names (owner's decision, 2026-11-16)

| What | Name |
|---|---|
| Series (all the collector games) | **Shortprint** |
| This game (Wildkin, Tidemark and Broken Reach in one world; the `tcg` build) | **Shortprint: The Pull** |
| The underlying engine | **Provenance** |

Why: "short print" is a hobby term for a card or comic printed in smaller numbers, true for both cards and comics, so it carries a series. "The Pull" is what you get from a pack. Provenance: every copy in the game keeps its own history (owners, condition, sales).

Rejected: **Pullbox** (a registered US trademark in comics, serial 88573610, and a live online pack-opening site called PullBox). Also considered: Backstock (backup choice), Firstprint, Dollar Bin, Wantlist, Gem Mint, Toploader, The Back Room, Cardboard Kingdom.

- Website address and itch.io name checks.
