## Artwork and corrections

TM-S001 is byte-for-byte unchanged from the approved delivery. Revised S003 is retained as the varied traditional-media baseline. The earlier S007 banner correction is retained, including Free Fleets' ship steering wheel. Twenty-six failed cells were locally corrected after subject review; all other 631 artwork files were preserved. Pixels outside the corrected paint regions remain identical to their original sheets. Twenty modified sheets have their original full-resolution sources in sources/original-sheets. local-corrections.json maps every replacement to its source proof and original card. A closer review cleared the lighthouse guard's halberd and the five-piglet family; those paintings were preserved.

## Practical limits

These are native returned digital illustrations. The full sheets are 1448×1086; exports are 468×351 or 472×354. No fabricated resolution or upscaling is claimed. Some peripheral details remain closer to edges than the central-80-percent target; use the supplied 4:3 crops and avoid further aggressive cropping. Fine incidental anatomy, rigging mechanics and tiny environmental marks remain subject to owner/integration review. Varied medium descriptions describe visual treatments, not physical originals or human artist attribution. No card frames, rules text, interfaces or gameplay files are included.

Exact physical subjects take precedence over generic directions when those conflict, including underwater vessels versus the generic vessel-on-water template. Approved Sentinel of Azure Keep, Roothorn Stag and Choir-Depth Leviathan files remain outside this queue and were not regenerated. Their unavailable concept files are not part of the 657 delivered assets. No upload to GitHub was performed.

## Using this delivery

The under30 ZIPs supersede the earlier larger final ZIPs, batch ZIPs and correction patches. Every delivery ZIP is strictly below 30,000,000 bytes. Extract all under30 ZIPs into the same folder: each uses tidemark-production/sheets, art, reports and sources. There are no conflicting image files between final parts. The global production-status.csv and quality-notes.md repeated in each part are identical. Per-part CSVs identify its sheets, occupied cells, blanks and integration status.
